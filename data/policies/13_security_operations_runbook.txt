Policy ID: IT-007
Title: Security Operations Runbook (Synthetic Training Policy)
Access Level: restricted
Incidents are rated P1, P2, P3 or P4 by the Security Operations Center. P1 incidents require an initial response within 15 minutes and P2 incidents within one hour. The on-call analyst must notify the support lead within 30 minutes of a P1 rating. Incident logs are retained for 180 days for investigation. Synthetic training content added by the project team.
