Policy ID: HR-007
Title: New Joiner Onboarding (Synthetic Training Policy)
Every new joiner is assigned a buddy for the first 30 days. Equipment and system accounts must be ready on the first working day. New joiners must complete their profile and emergency contact details within five working days. The line manager holds a check-in meeting at the end of the first week and again after 90 days. Synthetic training content added by the project team.
