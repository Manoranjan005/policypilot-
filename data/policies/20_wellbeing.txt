Policy ID: HR-012
Title: Wellbeing and Employee Assistance (Synthetic Training Policy)
Employees can use the confidential employee assistance service for counselling. Up to six free counselling sessions are available per year. The service is independent of the company and does not share personal details with managers. Managers should encourage team members to take regular breaks and should not contact them outside working hours except in emergencies. Synthetic training content added by the project team.
