Policy ID: HR-017
Title: Annual Performance Review (Synthetic Training Policy)
Every employee has a formal performance review once a year. The manager and employee agree on objectives at the start of the year and hold a mid-year check-in. Feedback must be based on facts and examples rather than opinions. The manager records the outcome in writing and both parties sign the record. Employees may add their own comments to the written record. Synthetic training content added by the project team.
