Policy ID: HR-018
Title: Mentoring and Coaching (Synthetic Training Policy)
Every manager acts as a mentor to their direct reports and holds a one-to-one meeting at least once a month. Employees may also request a mentor from outside their own team through the HR portal. Mentoring pairs are reviewed after six months. Guidance should be given in person or by video call rather than only in writing. Synthetic training content added by the project team.
