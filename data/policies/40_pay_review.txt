Policy ID: HR-022
Title: Pay Review Process (Synthetic Training Policy)
Pay is reviewed once a year in April. The company compares its pay levels with the market and aims to stay above the market average. Managers propose pay changes based on performance, skills and potential. Managers must explain the reason for the outcome to each employee in a one-to-one conversation. Pay information must not be shared with colleagues without the employee's agreement. Synthetic training content added by the project team.
