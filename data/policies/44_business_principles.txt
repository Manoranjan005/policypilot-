Title: Business Principles and Ethics Reporting (Synthetic Training Policy)
All employees must follow the company business principles, which cover child labour, human rights and fair treatment. Suppliers and outsourced service providers must be given the business principles and agree to follow them. Suspected breaches can be reported to the ethics mailbox, including anonymously. Retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith is treated as a serious breach. Synthetic training content added by the project team.
