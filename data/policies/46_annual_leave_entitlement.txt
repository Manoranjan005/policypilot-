Policy ID: HR-028
Title: Annual Leave Entitlement and Carry Forward (Synthetic Training Policy)
Full-time employees receive 24 paid working days of annual leave per year. Leave must be requested in the leave system and approved by the manager. Up to five unused days may be carried forward to the next year and must be used by 31 March. Leave of more than 15 consecutive working days needs written approval from the department head. Public holidays are not counted against annual leave. Synthetic training content added by the project team.
