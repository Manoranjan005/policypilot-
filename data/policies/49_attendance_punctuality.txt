Title: Attendance and Punctuality (Synthetic Training Policy)
Standard working hours are 09:00 to 18:00, with core hours from 10:00 to 16:00 when everyone should be available. Arriving more than 15 minutes after the start time counts as a late arrival. Three late arrivals in one calendar month lead to a discussion with the manager. Late arrivals caused by transport disruption are excused when reported to the manager.
