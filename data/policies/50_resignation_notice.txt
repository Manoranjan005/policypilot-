Title: Resignation and Notice Period (Synthetic Training Policy)
Resignation must be submitted in writing to the manager and HR. The notice period is 60 days. Leave may be taken during the notice period only with the written approval of the manager. Unused annual leave that is not taken is paid out on the last working day. Employees must hand over their work and return all company equipment and access badges by the last working day.
