Title: Same-Day and Short-Notice Leave (Synthetic Training Policy)
Leave requested with less than three working days' notice is short-notice leave. Short-notice leave, including leave for today or tomorrow, needs the manager's approval in the leave system. Same-day leave is approved only for emergencies or sickness. Tell your manager by phone or message before your working day starts. Synthetic training content added by the project team.
