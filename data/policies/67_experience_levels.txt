Policy ID: HR-045
Title: Experience Levels (Synthetic Training Policy)
Employees are placed in an experience level at hiring: entry level for 0 to 2 years of relevant experience, mid level for 3 to 6 years and senior level for 7 or more years. The level is based on relevant experience and the hiring assessment. Prior experience counts only when it is supported by experience letters. Pay within a level is decided case by case by the manager and HR. Synthetic training content added by the project team.
