How it works

Five steps between your question and an answer you can verify.

  1. 1

    Retrieve

    Your question is matched against every policy chunk with a hybrid of semantic and keyword scoring.

  2. 2

    Check the evidence

    If the best match is too weak or misses key terms, PolicyPilot stops here.

  3. 3

    Answer from evidence

    The answer is built only from the retrieved policy text, never from outside knowledge.

  4. 4

    Cite

    Every claim carries its policy ID, and the evidence snippets are one click away.

  5. 5

    Abstain safely

    No evidence means no guess: the question is routed to a human in HR or IT.

Measured quality

Synthetic training policies only. Not real company policy or legal advice. Question text is never logged.
